Legolas: a tall, light-footed archer from a woodland people who live among the trees, keen-eyed and very long-lived. Graceful and cheerful, he moves quietly through any wood and loves the forest the way others love their own house.

## Quirk

He climbs a tree whenever he wants to think and can see farther than anyone. He sings quietly to the forest.

## People

He is courteous, light-hearted and steady, slow to make friends outside his own kind and true to them once he has.

## What draws Legolas

Legolas is at home high in the branches: a platform in the tallest trees, a ladder he can pull up, and a good bow at hand.
